Who we are

Novi is a trading interface for digit contracts on synthetic indices, operated from Nairobi, Kenya. Where these terms say we or us, they mean Novi; you means the person holding the account.

Contact: support through the app, or the email address on your account confirmation. We answer within 30 minutes during working hours.

Opening an account

You must be 18 or over and legally able to enter a contract where you live. One account per person. The details you give us have to be true, and they have to be yours, an account opened in somebody else’s name, or funded from somebody else’s M-Pesa, will be closed and the balance returned to its source.

You are responsible for keeping your password to yourself. Tell us immediately if you think someone else has your account.

What a contract is

Each contract is an agreement between you and us on the last digit of a price series. You choose the instrument, the contract type and the stake. If the condition is met at settlement, we pay the stated payout. If it is not, the stake is lost. There is no position to hold, no margin, and nothing to owe beyond the stake.

Payouts are fixed at the moment you place the contract and shown before you confirm. They are priced so that we keep a margin at the true probability, which means that over enough contracts the expected return is negative. That is set out in Risk Disclosure, and we would rather you knew it than found out.

Money in and out

Deposits are credited when the payment provider confirms them, not when the request is made. Withdrawals are reviewed by a person and sent to the M-Pesa number on your verified profile. We may ask you to verify your identity before the first payout leaves your account.

We do not charge a fee to deposit or withdraw. Your provider might. A failed deposit is never charged, if money left your account and did not arrive, send us the reference and we will trace it.

When we would close an account

For fraud, for money that is not yours, for an attempt to interfere with the platform or its pricing, or where the law requires it. If we close an account for any other reason, the balance is yours and we will return it.

You can close yours whenever you like, from your profile or by asking us. We pay out what is in it.

What we do not promise

That the service is uninterrupted, that a price feed never stops, or that a trade you intended to place will always reach us. We will tell you when something is broken rather than let you find out mid-contract.

Nothing here is financial advice. We do not know your circumstances and we are not licensed to advise on them.

Changing these terms

If we change something that affects you, we will say so in the app before it takes effect, and the date at the top of this page will change. Continuing to trade after that means you accept the new version.
