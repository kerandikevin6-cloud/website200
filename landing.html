How it works

Three steps, and it is over in seconds.

A contract asks one question about the last digit of the next few ticks. You pick the answer, set a stake, and it settles itself.

01

Fund the account

M-Pesa, card or USDT. Mobile money lands in about two minutes and is quoted in your own currency, so you send the figure you expect to send.

02

Pick a contract

Even or odd, over or under a barrier, matches or differs from one digit. Every payout is on the button before you commit anything.

03

Watch it settle

One to ten ticks later it is done. Wins are credited immediately, and every contract stays on your ledger with its entry and exit price.

FAQ

The questions worth asking first.

What is a synthetic index?

A price series generated by a published random process rather than by a real-world market. Nothing external moves it, no news, no earnings, no opening bell. The number in the name is its volatility: Volatility 10 moves gently, Volatility 100 moves ten times as hard.

Can I actually make money doing this?

Every contract is priced slightly below fair value, so the expected return on each one starts negative. Over enough contracts that margin is what you pay to trade. People do finish sessions ahead, but no strategy on this platform turns a negative expectation positive, and anyone telling you otherwise is selling something.

Is there a free way to try it?

Yes. Every account opens with a demo balance running on the same feed, the same pricing and the same contracts, with virtual funds. Place a hundred contracts there and look at the total before you fund anything.

How fast are withdrawals?

Payouts are reviewed and sent within the hour to the M-Pesa number or USDT address on your profile. Identity verification is a one-time check before your first payout leaves the account.

What does the referral programme pay?

Not cash. Each referral who funds an account raises your payout tier, which lifts what every winning contract pays you, up to a cap that deliberately stays below the house margin. It makes your own trading cheaper rather than paying you to recruit.

What happens if I lose connection mid-contract?

The contract settles on the feed regardless of whether your screen is open. The result and the credit are on your ledger when you come back.

Trading synthetic indices carries a high level of risk and is not suitable for everyone. You can lose your full stake.
