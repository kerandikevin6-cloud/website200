Security

How your money and your account are held.

What actually protects a balance here, described rather than claimed.

Scroll

Balances cannot be written by a request

No route in our API can change a balance. Money moves only through database functions that credit an account and write the matching ledger entry in the same transaction, so a balance and its history can never disagree.

The database grants no UPDATE on accounts and no INSERT on ledger entries to anyone, including a signed-in customer. Even a leaked key could not write itself money.

A deposit is verified, not believed

When a payment provider tells us a deposit succeeded, we do not take that message at its word. We ask their API what happened and credit from the answer. A replayed or forged notification changes nothing.

Every deposit is recorded before the provider is called, so a payment that succeeds while our reply is lost still finds a record to settle against. Nothing is stranded.

Payouts are reviewed by a person

There is no automatic payout endpoint. A withdrawal request holds the funds immediately, so the same balance cannot be requested twice, then waits for a human to approve it. An automatic payout route is the most valuable thing an attacker can find in a trading product, and the wait costs an honest customer under an hour.

Your account

Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider and are never stored or seen by us. Sessions expire, and signing out clears them on that device. If you think someone has your password, change it and tell support, we can end every session on the account immediately.

We will never ask you for your password, your M-Pesa PIN, or a code sent to your phone. Anyone who does is not us.

What we would tell you

If a security problem affected your account or your money, we would tell you what happened, what it touched and what we did about it, without waiting for the story to be tidy. Report anything that looks wrong to support.
