Risk

What you can lose, stated plainly.

Read this before you fund an account. It is short, and none of it is hedged.

You can lose the money you put in

Every contract risks its whole stake. There is no partial loss on a digit contract: it either pays out or it does not. Money put into this platform should be money you can lose entirely without it changing anything about your life.

The odds are known, and they are against you

Each digit 0-9 is equally likely, so the true probability of every contract is fixed and knowable. Payouts are set below that fair price: Even/Odd pays 1.953× on a 50% chance where a fair price would be 2.0×. About 2.3% of every stake is the cost of trading.

That gap does not average out. It compounds across every contract you place, and no timing rule can overcome it, because each tick is independent of the last.

A high win rate is not an edge

Differs wins nine times in ten and still loses money over a long enough run, because the one loss is larger than the nine wins combined. Judge any approach by expected return per contract, never by how often it wins.

Loss multipliers are the fastest way to lose everything

Doubling after a loss feels like a guaranteed recovery. Six losses at ×2 makes the seventh stake 64 times the first, and a run of six is ordinary. It trades a high chance of a small win for a small chance of losing the whole balance. The tool exists here because people ask for it; that is not a recommendation.

Synthetic indices are not real markets

The price series is generated by a published random process. Nothing external moves it, no news, no earnings, no market hours. Analysis that works on real assets has nothing to attach to here.

It also never closes, which removes the natural stopping point other markets impose. Set your own: target profit, target loss and session limits exist for this.

If it has stopped being a choice

Chasing losses, trading money meant for something else, or hiding it from people close to you are the signs that matter. Responsible Trading has the limits and the self-exclusion tools, and support can lock an account at your request without argument.
