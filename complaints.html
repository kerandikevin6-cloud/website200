Complaints

Something went wrong. Here is how it gets fixed.

A complaint is not an inconvenience to us. It is how we find out what is broken.

Scroll

Tell us

Message support in the app, or reply to any email we have sent you. Include the reference of the payment or contract if there is one, every deposit, payout and trade has one, and it lets us find the exact record instead of guessing.

Lucy answers support, and will acknowledge within 30 minutes during working hours.

What happens next

Within 30 minutes: we acknowledge, and tell you who is handling it.

Within 2 working days: an answer, or an explanation of what we are still checking and when you will hear again.

Within 14 days: a final position in writing, including what we found and what we are doing about it.

If money is involved

A deposit that left your account and did not arrive is traced with the provider, and you are not left waiting on that trace before we talk to you. If the error is ours, we put it right and say so. If it is not, we show you what the provider told us.

If you are not satisfied

Say so, and it is escalated past the person handling it. We will put our final position in writing so that you can take it further, and we will tell you where to take it rather than leaving you to find out.
